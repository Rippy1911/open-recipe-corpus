# Code of conduct

This project collects recipes from many kitchens and languages. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests,
reviews and translations, is expected to:

- be kind and assume good faith, including towards people writing in a second language;
- criticise the recipe, the translation or the code, never the person;
- respect every cuisine and food tradition, and the people who cook it;
- credit other people's work, and only submit photos, text and translations you have the right to share
  ([`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md));
- keep other people's personal details out of the repository.

Not accepted: harassment, insults, slurs, sexual content, threats, spam, and publishing someone's private
information.

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who do not follow
this. To report something, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment, or contact the maintainer through their
GitHub profile if it should stay private. Reports are read by the maintainer only.
