**Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International** licence (CC BY-SA 4.0).

- **Full legal code:** <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode>
- **Summary:** <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>

You may copy, share and adapt the data for any purpose, including commercially, as long as you:

1. **Give credit.** Keep each recipe's credit line, a link to its source page, and a note that it was changed.
   [`ATTRIBUTION.md`](ATTRIBUTION.md) shows how.
2. **Share alike.** Release your adaptations under CC BY-SA 4.0 too.
3. **Add no restrictions** that would stop others from doing what this licence allows.

## Where the recipes come from

| Source | Licence of the source | Licence here |
|---|---|---|
| `pl-wikibooks`: pl.wikibooks.org, *Książka kucharska* | CC BY-SA 4.0 | CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| `en-wikibooks`: en.wikibooks.org, *Cookbook* (none in this release yet) | CC BY-SA 4.0 | CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| `pl-wikisource`: public-domain cookbooks on pl.wikisource.org | public domain | CC BY-SA 4.0 for our structured version. The original book text stays in the public domain. |

**Photos** contributed under `photos/` carry their own licence and author, which are stated next to each photo.

**AI images** under `ai-images/` and `ai-recipe-images/` are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0
(<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>). Each one is marked as AI-generated in its `.json` sidecar
and in its embedded metadata (IPTC digital source type `trainedAlgorithmicMedia`). Keep that mark, and do not present
them as photographs of a recipe.
