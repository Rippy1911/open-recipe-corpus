# AI recipe images

Step-by-step illustrations of AI-written recipes, one folder per recipe: `<step>.webp` for each step, and
`hero.webp`, a crop of one step used as the recipe's picture. Each `.json` names the `recipe` and the `step` and
says `ai_generated: true`. They are CC0 1.0 and are marked as AI-generated in their embedded metadata (IPTC
`trainedAlgorithmicMedia`). They are not photographs, and the recipes themselves are not part of this release.
